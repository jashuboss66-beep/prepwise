## Source and licence

The Hub questions are original project content. Every seeded record is tagged `Original: Prepwise Placement Learning Hub` or `Original: Prepwise project`, with licence metadata stating that the project content is original and no third-party question text was reused. No IndiaBIX or other copyrighted questions were scraped or copied. Reference links are optional metadata and are not represented as question sources.

## Validation performed

The backend test suite checks the seeded category totals, prompt uniqueness, four-option structure, valid answer indices, explanation presence, topic/difficulty metadata, and source/licence metadata. The generated Hub question list also passes its seed-time structural validator. These checks validate structure and consistency; they are **not** an independent subject-matter or editorial review of every answer.
